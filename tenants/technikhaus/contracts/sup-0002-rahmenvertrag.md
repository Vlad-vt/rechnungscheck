# Framework supply agreement RV-TEC-002

**Buyer:** Technikhaus Retail GmbH
**Supplier:** Northgate Components GmbH (SUP-0002), Düsseldorf
**Effective:** 1 January 2026, open-ended, three months' notice to the end of a quarter.

## 1 Prices
The net prices in the buyer's price agreements apply. Price changes require written notice at
least 60 days in advance. Unannounced price increases are not accepted; the buyer pays
the agreed price.

## 2 Quantities and short deliveries
No quantity or price tolerance applies; every deviation must be clarified.
For short deliveries only the quantity actually delivered is paid. The supplier issues a credit
note for over-invoiced quantities without being asked.

## 3 Invoicing
Invoices must be sent as structured e-invoices (XRechnung or ZUGFeRD, profile EN 16931 or higher)
to rechnungseingang@technikhaus.example. Every invoice must state the purchase order number and the delivery note number.
For serialised goods the serial numbers per line must be stated on delivery note and invoice.

## 4 Payment
Payment term 45 days net from receipt of invoice. No early-payment discount.
Changes of bank details only take effect after written confirmation by the supplier's management
and a call-back confirmation by the buyer.

## 5 Credit notes
Credit notes must be issued within 14 days of request and must reference the original
invoice number.

## 6 Shelf life and traceability
Serial numbers must be complete and unique; missing serial numbers lead to clarification.

## 7 Miscellaneous
There are no oral side agreements. Place of jurisdiction is the buyer's registered office.
